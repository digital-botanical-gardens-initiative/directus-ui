Collection treemap

Areas represent resolved field samples, grouped by their linked Taxa family, genus and exact identification. Choose all projects or a botanical garden/project; click a rectangle to explore its descendants. Names and ancestry come only from Taxa, never from field-name text.

Only resolved samples appear. Identifications above family rank are grouped under “Without a family in Taxa”; identifications at family or genus rank remain visible at that rank. Hover or focus a small tile for its full name and count.
