# Manual browser checks

Run `python3 -m http.server 8000` from the repository root and open <http://localhost:8000/>. Use the committed snapshots; you do **not** need a Directus account. IDs below are examples from the currently committed snapshot and may change after a refresh. Third-party pages or map tiles may be unavailable: check the local state, constructed link and fallback without claiming remote results.

- **Overview and source:** Confirm the five garden cards appear, the data-freshness panel distinguishes a sample fetch from a completed six-export refresh, and a garden card opens its filtered map. The garden progress plot should satisfy cumulative **Collected ≥ Extracted ≥ Profiled** while showing each field sample in only one bar segment.
- **Samples and taxonomy:** In `#samples`, search `Rubiaceae` (including a partial name), filter by project, recorded plant organ and **Profiled samples only**, and switch Cards → List → Map without losing the sample set. Lists sort/filter the whole matched set before pagination. On the map, click a cluster once per zoom level; at maximum zoom every recorded location is individually clickable, including coincident points. Records without coordinates remain in Cards/List. A family or upper-taxon page must include its Directus Taxa descendants; an exact taxon drilldown must not merge spellings.
- **Sample and species:** Open `#sample/65650` (currently `dbgi_003141`), then its *Ruscus aculeatus* species page. Check recorded subject icons, source-linked IDs, CoL hierarchy, sample-specific extraction/MS status, precise location and weather caveats. Species-level MS evidence must not claim that every field sample was profiled. Check `#species/556` (*Lonicera xylosteum*) → `dbgi_002628` → its dried material, extracts, aliquots and two separate MS_Data analyses; inspect an unresolved or genus-resolved sample for appropriately absent species links.
- **Collecting and curation:** In `#collectors`, choose a collector; Cards/List/Map must share only their linked field samples, while the species summary groups by Directus Taxa ancestry. In `#inaturalist`, distinguish community-ID disagreement candidates from records with only an observer ID or comments; photo captions are not community determinations.
- **Network:** In `#network`, search `dbgi_003185` and inspect its sample metadata on the right. Expand it: the sample can simultaneously connect to a taxon, observation subject, project, garden and location. Taxon nodes lead to actual Directus parent/child records; selecting or focusing a node must not fabricate a taxonomy edge.
- **Compare MS:** In `#compare-ms`, search `dbgi_003141` and add **only** MS_Data `#2807` to group A; add a file from a different sample to B. Check `usi`/`usi2`, method-derived +/− polarity, BPI default and preserved XIC m/z across changes. Launch a LOTUS search for either *selected* species (or unscoped), calculate a formula's adduct with ChemCalc, then copy the ion m/z into GNPS2's XIC form. Do not interpret a literature metabolite hit or XIC peak as identification. With no group-A file, the calculator and GNPS viewer should be absent. Species pages offer LOTUS but no adduct calculator.
- **Publication:** On the deployed Pages URL, verify `assets/js/app.js`, `assets/css/styles.css`, `assets/dbgi-metasequoia.webp` and `data/samples.json` load from the same Pages origin (including when hosted under `/directus-ui/`). The footer must open the local `LICENSING.md`, and both it and `LICENSE` must be in the Pages artifact. `data/refresh.json` appears only after a complete credentialed refresh. The artifact must not expose `.env`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `docs/` or a Directus token. If LOTUS, GNPS2, ChemCalc, OpenStreetMap or iNaturalist cannot load, the dashboard's local records and external fallback links should remain usable.
