# Manual browser checks

Run `python3 scripts/stage_site.py --site dbgi --output site-dbgi` and `python3 scripts/stage_site.py --site emi --output site-emi`, then serve the repository with `python3 -m http.server 8000`. Preview <http://localhost:8000/site-dbgi/> and <http://localhost:8000/site-emi/>; no Directus account is needed when using committed snapshots. IDs below are examples from the currently committed data and may change after refresh. Third-party pages or map tiles may be unavailable: check local state and fallback without claiming remote results.

- **Overview and source:** DBGI displays five garden cards, and each opens its filtered map. EMI displays its own name/logo and locally hosted Earth Metabolome hero image (not the DBGI banner); its landing page shows one collapsed DBGI card plus Fung, Champex gradient, ViNEsh, Macedonia and Manaslu cards. Expand DBGI to reveal the same five garden cards and click one; click the DBGI parent to see all five gardens together. Each of the other five project cards must filter to only its actual Directus project code. Expand the remaining-projects disclosure to find every other source project, including `emi`. The data-freshness panel distinguishes a sample fetch from a completed six-export refresh; the garden progress plot satisfies cumulative **Collected ≥ Extracted ≥ Profiled** with each sample in one segment.
- **Samples and taxonomy:** In both sites, search `Rubiaceae` (including a partial name), filter by garden/project, recorded plant organ and **Profiled samples only**, and switch Cards → List → Map without losing the sample set. DBGI must offer **exactly five garden groups**, including Neuchâtel samples from both `jbn` and `jbn-new` without separate project options, direct project fields or other-project records. EMI retains all project records and original project codes. Lists sort/filter the whole matched set before pagination. On the map, click a cluster once per zoom level; at maximum zoom every recorded location is individually clickable, including coincident points. Records without coordinates remain in Cards/List. A family or upper-taxon page must include its Directus Taxa descendants; an exact taxon drilldown must not merge spellings.
- **Sample and species:** Open `#sample/65650` (currently `dbgi_003141`), then its *Ruscus aculeatus* species page. Check recorded subject icons, source-linked IDs, CoL hierarchy, sample-specific extraction/MS status, precise location and weather caveats. Species-level MS evidence must not claim that every field sample was profiled. Check `#species/556` (*Lonicera xylosteum*) → `dbgi_002628` → its dried material, extracts, aliquots and two separate MS_Data analyses; inspect an unresolved or genus-resolved sample for appropriately absent species links.
- **Collecting and curation:** In `#collectors`, choose a collector; Cards/List/Map must share only their linked field samples, while the species summary groups by Directus Taxa ancestry. In `#inaturalist`, distinguish community-ID disagreement candidates from records with only an observer ID or comments; photo captions are not community determinations.
- **Network:** In `#network`, search `dbgi_003185` and inspect its sample metadata on the right. On DBGI, expanding the sample should connect directly to its garden, taxon, observation subject and location, **without an individual project node**. EMI also links its recorded project to the garden. Taxon nodes lead to actual Directus parent/child records; selecting or focusing a node must not fabricate a taxonomy edge.
- **Compare MS:** In `#compare-ms`, search `dbgi_003141` and add **only** MS_Data `#2807` to group A; add a file from a different sample to B. Check `usi`/`usi2`, method-derived +/− polarity, BPI default and preserved XIC m/z across changes. Launch a LOTUS search for either *selected* species (or unscoped), calculate a formula's adduct with ChemCalc, then copy the ion m/z into GNPS2's XIC form. Do not interpret a literature metabolite hit or XIC peak as identification. With no group-A file, the calculator and GNPS viewer should be absent. Species pages offer LOTUS but no adduct calculator.
- **Publication:** On each deployed Pages URL, verify `assets/js/app.js`, `assets/css/styles.css` and `data/samples.json` load from the same Pages origin under `/directus-ui/`. DBGI serves `assets/dbgi-metasequoia.webp` and its icon; EMI serves `assets/emi-ecuador-river.webp` and `assets/emi-logo.png`, with EMI identity in HTML title, header and footer. DBGI `data/samples.json` must have only five canonical garden project IDs; its public collector, processing, MS and iNaturalist snapshots must reference only those samples, and its garden catalog must not expose individual project aliases. EMI must retain the complete project snapshot and original codes. The footer must open local `LICENSING.md`, and both it and `LICENSE` must be in each Pages artifact. `data/refresh.json` appears only after a complete credentialed refresh. Neither artifact may expose `.env`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `docs/` or a Directus token. If external services cannot load, local records and fallback links should remain usable.
